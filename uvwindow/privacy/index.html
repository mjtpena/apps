UV Window: Sunscreen Timer privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Datachain Consulting Pty Ltd ("we", "us") makes UV Window: Sunscreen Timer.

The short version

UV Window: Sunscreen Timer has no account and no ads, and we run no server that holds your data. What you enter stays on your phone, except for the lookups listed below.

Data on your phone

What you enter is stored in a database on your phone. We can't see it. If you export a backup, the file goes where you send it, for example Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. That service's own privacy policy then applies. If you delete UV Window: Sunscreen Timer, the data on your phone goes with it, unless you saved a backup.

Lookups that leave your phone

When you use the features below, UV Window: Sunscreen Timer sends a request from your phone straight to the service named. We don't run these services and we never receive the request.

  • MET Norway (api.met.no): sends the place you choose, rounded to about 1 km, and the app name with its support page in the request header.

Each request carries only what is listed. No name, account or advertising ID is attached. The service sees your phone's network address, as any website does. Its own privacy policy applies to what it does with the request.

Permissions

UV Window: Sunscreen Timer asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change it later in your phone's settings.

  • Location: UV Window uses your location to show the UV forecast where you are.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. UV Window: Sunscreen Timer checks your purchase with the store on your phone. Apple and Google send us sales reports and, if you have agreed on your phone to share diagnostics with developers, crash and performance reports. We use them to count sales and fix problems. We have no way to link them to anything you enter in UV Window: Sunscreen Timer.

Children

UV Window: Sunscreen Timer doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no account and asks for no name, age or contact details.

Your rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. If you live in Australia, the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with privacy laws, you can still write to us to ask what we hold or to raise a concern.

The services named above have their own privacy policies and hold their own data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
