Aquarium Log: Tank Tracker privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Datachain Consulting Pty Ltd ("we", "us") makes Aquarium Log: Tank Tracker.

The short version

We do not collect your personal information. Aquarium Log: Tank Tracker has no account, no ads and no analytics, and we run no server that holds your data. What you enter stays on your phone.

Data on your phone

What you enter is stored in a database on your phone. We can't see it. If you export a backup, the file goes where you send it, for example Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. That service's own privacy policy then applies. If you delete Aquarium Log: Tank Tracker, the data on your phone goes with it, unless you saved a backup.

Permissions

Aquarium Log: Tank Tracker asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change it later in your phone's settings.

  • Camera: Photograph a test strip or your tank.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. Aquarium Log: Tank Tracker checks your purchase with the store on your phone. Apple and Google send us sales reports and, if you have agreed on your phone to share diagnostics with developers, crash and performance reports. We use them to count sales and fix problems. We have no way to link them to anything you enter in Aquarium Log: Tank Tracker.

Children

Aquarium Log: Tank Tracker doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no account and asks for no name, age or contact details.

Your rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. If you live in Australia, the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with privacy laws, you can still write to us to ask what we hold or to raise a concern.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
