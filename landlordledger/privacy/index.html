Landlord Ledger: Rental Tax privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Datachain Consulting Pty Ltd ("we", "us") makes Landlord Ledger: Rental Tax.

The short version

Landlord Ledger: Rental Tax has no account and no ads, and we run no server that holds your data. What you enter stays on your phone. The only data that leaves it is the diagnostics that Google ML Kit sends to Google, described below.

Data on your phone

What you enter is stored in a database on your phone. We can't see it. If you export a backup, the file goes where you send it, for example Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. That service's own privacy policy then applies. If you delete Landlord Ledger: Rental Tax, the data on your phone goes with it, unless you saved a backup.

Google ML Kit diagnostics

Landlord Ledger: Rental Tax uses Google ML Kit to read text in photos on your phone. The text, images and audio you use are processed on the phone and are not sent anywhere. ML Kit itself sends basic diagnostics to Google: device model and operating system version, the app's name and version, and performance figures such as how long a task took. Google says this data is encrypted in transit and not shared with third parties. We can't turn it off. Google's description is at ML Kit data disclosure.

Processing on your phone

Processing for text in photos happens on your phone. The photos, audio and text you use for it are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

Landlord Ledger: Rental Tax asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change it later in your phone's settings.

  • Camera: Photograph receipts and agent statements for your rental records.
  • Face ID: Lock your rental finances.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. Landlord Ledger: Rental Tax checks your purchase with the store on your phone. Apple and Google send us sales reports and, if you have agreed on your phone to share diagnostics with developers, crash and performance reports. We use them to count sales and fix problems. We have no way to link them to anything you enter in Landlord Ledger: Rental Tax.

Children

Landlord Ledger: Rental Tax doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no account and asks for no name, age or contact details.

Your rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. If you live in Australia, the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with privacy laws, you can still write to us to ask what we hold or to raise a concern.

Google holds the ML Kit diagnostics under its own privacy policy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
