Um Counter: Speaking Coach support

Email us at mjtpena@outlook.com. We read every message.

What to include

  • What you were doing and what happened.
  • Your phone model and its iOS or Android version.
  • The app version: open Settings, then About, in Um Counter: Speaking Coach.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? On your phone only. We have no server and no accounts. Export a backup from Settings before you change phones or delete the app.
  • How do I move to a new phone? Export a backup on the old phone, send the file to the new phone, and import it from Settings.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Open Settings, then Restore purchases, with the same Apple account or Google Play account you bought with.
  • How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Ask them directly.
