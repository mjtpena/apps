Skin Diary: Routine & Photos privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Datachain Consulting Pty Ltd ("we", "us") makes Skin Diary: Routine & Photos.

The short version

Skin Diary: Routine & Photos has no account and no ads, and we run no server that holds your data. What you enter stays on your phone, except for the lookups listed below.

Data on your phone

What you enter is stored in a database on your phone. We can't see it. If you export a backup, the file goes where you send it, for example Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. That service's own privacy policy then applies. If you delete Skin Diary: Routine & Photos, the data on your phone goes with it, unless you saved a backup.

Lookups that leave your phone

When you use the features below, Skin Diary: Routine & Photos sends a request from your phone straight to the service named. We don't run these services and we never receive the request.

  • Open Beauty Facts (world.openbeautyfacts.org): sends the product barcode or search term you look up.

Each request carries only what is listed. No name, account or advertising ID is attached. The service sees your phone's network address, as any website does. Its own privacy policy applies to what it does with the request.

Google ML Kit diagnostics

Skin Diary: Routine & Photos uses Google ML Kit to read barcodes on your phone. The text, images and audio you use are processed on the phone and are not sent anywhere. ML Kit itself sends basic diagnostics to Google: device model and operating system version, the app's name and version, and performance figures such as how long a task took. Google says this data is encrypted in transit and not shared with third parties. We can't turn it off. Google's description is at ML Kit data disclosure.

Processing on your phone

Processing for barcodes happens on your phone. The photos, audio and text you use for it are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

Skin Diary: Routine & Photos asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change it later in your phone's settings.

  • Camera: Skin Diary uses the camera to take progress photos. Photos are stored only inside the app on this phone.
  • Face ID: Unlock your private progress photos.
  • Saving to Photos: Save a progress photo you choose to your Photos

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. Skin Diary: Routine & Photos checks your purchase with the store on your phone. Apple and Google send us sales reports and, if you have agreed on your phone to share diagnostics with developers, crash and performance reports. We use them to count sales and fix problems. We have no way to link them to anything you enter in Skin Diary: Routine & Photos.

Children

Skin Diary: Routine & Photos doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no account and asks for no name, age or contact details.

Your rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. If you live in Australia, the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with privacy laws, you can still write to us to ask what we hold or to raise a concern.

The services named above have their own privacy policies and hold their own data.

Google holds the ML Kit diagnostics under its own privacy policy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
